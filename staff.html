Add MIC Staff to the Home Screen

An iPad only shows notifications for apps on its Home Screen. You do this once, and it takes a few seconds.

  1. Tap the Share button: the square with an arrow, at the top of the screen.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Close Safari, open MIC Staff from the Home Screen, and sign in there.
