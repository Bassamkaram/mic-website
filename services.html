→ Services

Operating support
for growing companies.

Beyond workspaces and programs, MIC operates a set of professional services for founders and growing teams who need expertise on tap — without the overhead of building it in-house.

HR Services

From your first hire to your fifteenth, we handle the operational backbone of building a team in Lebanon — contracts, payroll, NSSF, performance frameworks, and the conversations nobody warns you about.

  • Recruitment & technical screening
  • Lebanese-compliant contracts & payroll setup
  • NSSF, taxes, and end-of-service administration
  • Performance review frameworks
  • Founder-employee & co-founder mediation

Business Advisory

Fractional senior operators who've built and sold companies, on call for the questions you'd want to ask a wise board member — pricing, packaging, channel strategy, when to pivot, when not to.

  • Strategic planning & OKR design
  • Pricing & commercial model review
  • Go-to-market & channel strategy
  • Financial modeling & unit economics
  • Board preparation & investor narratives

Team Augmentation

When you need talent now and don't have the runway for a full-time hire, we deploy vetted engineers, designers, and operators from the MIC network — embedded with your team for as long as it takes.

  • Senior engineers (full-stack, mobile, ML)
  • Product designers & UX researchers
  • Growth marketers & content operators
  • Fractional CTOs, CFOs, & CPOs
  • Project-based or ongoing engagements

Virtual Offices

A Lebanese business address, mail handling, and on-demand workspace access for diaspora founders, remote teams, and international companies who need a real presence here without leasing real estate.

  • Business address at the campus
  • Mail & package receipt + forwarding
  • Local phone line with call routing
  • 10 hours/month of coworking & meeting rooms
  • Optional: company registration assistance

Venture Capital Funding

For Acceleration-track companies and select graduates, MIC operates an evergreen capital vehicle making pre-seed and seed checks alongside regional and diaspora co-investors.

  • Check sizes: $50K – $250K (pre-seed & seed)
  • Co-investment with regional & diaspora VCs
  • Investor introductions for later rounds
  • Cap table & SAFE structuring support
  • Post-investment operating support
