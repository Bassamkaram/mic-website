Three Programs.
One Path.

From the night you decide to try, to the day you raise your first round. Three programs designed to take your idea or business to the next and next levels…

No. 01 · Pre-MVP

Pre-Incubation

12 weeks

You have a problem you care about and a hunch about the solution. We help you formulate it, stress-test it and validate it before you spend a year of your life on the wrong thing. Twelve weeks of structured discovery, mentorship, feasibility, business modeling and prototype sprints.

Duration
12 weeks
Cohort size
Up to 12 teams
Commitment
2 days/week
Outcome
Validated solution, first prototype & pitch deck
Cost
Hot desk membership for 12 weeks — all benefits included
What you'll work on
  • Problem and solution framing
  • Feasibility study
  • Market discovery and understanding
  • Prototyping
  • Storytelling and pitch fundamentals
  • Weekly 1:1s with a senior advisor
  • Cohort demo day at week 12
Who it's for
  • First-time founders with an idea but no product
  • Domain experts considering a venture but unsure where to start
  • Individuals or teams of 1–3 who can commit to two days a week on campus
No. 02 · MVP-stage

Incubation

6 months

You have a valid model or working prototype and already did your first conversations with potential customers. Now you need to build, sell and start operating, without the wheels falling off. Six months of intensive on-campus work covering product, go-to-market, operations and capacity-building.

Duration
6 months
Cohort size
Up to 8 teams
Commitment
Full-time, on campus
Outcome
Launch-ready product & first paying customers
Cost
Hot desk membership for 6 months per team member — all benefits included
What you'll work on
  • Product development
  • Pricing & business model development
  • Go-to-market & "first 100" customers
  • Legal, accounting and taxation clinics
  • Investment pitch
  • Fundraising readiness & investor introductions
Who it's for
  • Individuals or teams of 2–5 with a working prototype and early traction
  • Founders ready to commit full-time for six months
  • Companies targeting Lebanese, regional, or global markets from Lebanon
No. 03 · Revenue-stage

Acceleration

12 months

You have product-market fit and customers paying you already. Now you're deciding what to scale, how fast and where. Twelve months of operating discipline, capital strategy and expansion support. Built for founders ready to write their Series A readiness story.

Duration
12 months
Cohort size
Up to 6 companies
Commitment
Flexible, milestone-driven
Outcome
Scaled ops & Series A readiness
Cost
Private office membership for 12 months — all benefits included
What you'll work on
  • Operating playbooks & org design
  • Hiring & leadership coaching
  • Financial modeling & capital strategy
  • Regional market entry (GCC, MENA, diaspora)
  • Investor & board readiness
  • Network access to capital partners
Who it's for
  • Revenue-generating companies past $50K ARR
  • Teams with clear product-market fit
  • Founders preparing for or actively raising institutional capital
