Building Lebanon's
innovation campus.

MIC is a workspace, a community and a launchpad. We exist to give Lebanese founders, freelancers and growing teams the floor space, the company and the operating support to build serious companies from here. Not from somewhere else.

01 Our mission

Lebanon doesn't need more talk about its talent. It needs places where that talent can show up and do work.

For years, Lebanese founders have built remarkable companies. They've also, mostly, built them from abroad. The reasons are well-rehearsed: infrastructure, cost, capital and signal. MIC is built on the conviction that enough of that has changed, and enough hasn't, that a real campus with reliable services and a serious operating tempo can move the equation.

We're not a co-working space with a startup label on top. We're an operating platform: workspaces, structured programs, professional services and a calendar of events that turns isolated work into a community of work.

02 The campus

A building with a second life.

MIC occupies the Collège de la Sagesse Jdeide, Theater Building in Jdeideh, Mount Lebanon. A generous, high-ceilinged structure with the kind of bones modern offices rarely get. Ground floor through fifth floor, with two floors operated by MIC and three retained by existing tenants who'll continue serving the neighborhood.
