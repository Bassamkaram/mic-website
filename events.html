01 Recurring programming

What's on, most weeks.

Six recurring formats designed to keep founders connected, learning, and shipping. Coverage posted here after each event.

Every Friday · 12:30 PM

Founder Fridays

A standing lunch for campus members — one guest founder shares the most useful and most painful things of their business, then it's open-table conversation.

Weekly · members only — Hosted by MIC staff
Every Tuesday · 7:00 PM

Pitch Nights

Five founders, five minutes each, five minutes of feedback from the room. Open to the public — a low-stakes way to sharpen the pitch before it matters.

Weekly · open to all — Free, RSVP required
Last Thursday of the month · 6:30 PM

Industry Deep-Dives

Half-day intensives on a single vertical: agritech, fintech, cleantech, creator economy. Operators in the room, not pundits.

Monthly · open application — Ticketed
Bi-weekly · Wednesdays

Investor Office Hours

Active angels and fund partners take 25-minute slots with program members. Real conversations — pitch, feedback, follow-up if there's fit.

Bi-weekly · program members — By application
Quarterly · Saturdays

Open Studio

The campus opens its doors to anyone curious about it. Walk the floors, see what teams are building, meet the people. Drinks on us.

Quarterly · open application — Ticketed
Quarterly · End of each cohort

Cohort Demo Days

Graduating cohorts pitch the room: investors, customers, press and the incoming cohort. Founders pitching for real, not for practice.

Quarterly · semi-public — Curated guest list
