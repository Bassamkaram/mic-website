→ Workshops

Short, hands-on,
built to be useful.

One to two-day sessions led by practitioners who've shipped what they're teaching. Workshops at MIC are designed for founders, freelancers, and growing teams who need a specific skill to land in a specific week — not a semester from now.

Product & Strategy

From a vague idea to a defensible product bet. Hands-on sessions on customer discovery, roadmapping, and the kind of strategic clarity that survives contact with reality.

  • Customer discovery & problem validation
  • Roadmapping & prioritization frameworks
  • MVP scoping & build-vs-buy decisions
  • Product-market fit indicators
  • Pricing & packaging fundamentals

Growth & Marketing

The mechanics behind real traction. Practical workshops on content, channels, and the experiments that move metrics — not the ones that just look good in a deck.

  • Content strategy & SEO foundations
  • Paid acquisition & channel testing
  • Growth experiments & analytics setup
  • Brand voice & messaging hierarchies
  • Funnel analysis & retention tactics

Fundraising

What a serious round actually looks like. Sessions on pitch craft, term sheets, and the operating habits that make founders investable long before the first conversation.

  • Pitch deck workshop & live critique
  • Financial modeling for early-stage rounds
  • Term sheets, SAFEs, and convertible notes
  • Investor outreach & relationship building
  • Due diligence preparation

Operations & Scaling

The unglamorous machinery behind companies that don't break when they grow. Workshops on hiring, systems, and the operating cadences that turn intention into outcome.

  • Hiring playbooks & founder-led recruiting
  • OKRs, metrics, and goal-setting frameworks
  • Operating cadences & weekly rhythms
  • Process design & documentation
  • Building a remote-friendly operating model

Brand & Design

Design fundamentals for non-designers and brand strategy for teams who've been winging it. Sessions on identity, UX, and the visual instincts that make products feel considered.

  • Brand identity foundations & positioning
  • UX/UI fundamentals for non-designers
  • Design systems & component thinking
  • Working effectively with designers
  • Critique & design review craft

Sales & Customer Success

B2B sales fundamentals for founders who've never carried a quota, and customer success patterns for teams trying to keep what they win. Practical, role-played, and direct.

  • Customer interviews that surface real signal
  • B2B sales motions & deal qualification
  • Discovery calls & objection handling
  • Onboarding & activation playbooks
  • Retention, expansion, and churn diagnostics
→ How it works

Practical, paid, and
built for working schedules.

Format

One or two days

Most workshops run Friday afternoon through Saturday, structured as half-day blocks with hands-on exercises rather than lectures. Cohorts cap at 16 to keep the room conversational.

Who teaches

Working practitioners

Workshops are led by operators currently doing the work — senior product managers, growth marketers, fund partners, and operators from the MIC and partner network.

Access

Members + public

MIC members get priority registration and discounted rates. Most workshops are open to the public; some are reserved for active program participants.
